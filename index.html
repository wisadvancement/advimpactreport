Anniversary Giving Impact Report

Last year, we unified philanthropy under the banner of 60th Anniversary Giving, focusing support where it matters most.

  • Le Centre Brown — fully funded
  • Strengthening teaching and learning
  • Expanding access through financial aid

Annual Impact of Philanthropy

The below investments were made possible in part by your giving. Thank you!

$4.8M

Financial aid

WIS’s financial aid budget provided an average grant of $37,000 in 2025–26 — made possible in large part by the generosity of our community.

$1.6M

Expanded food service

Teachers and staff now receive meals as part of their employment, freeing up time and resources to focus on their practice.

$200K

Tregaron solar panels

WIS installed 185 panels on the Middle School roof. The panels will generate about 74,000 kWh annually, enough to power seven homes.

$170K

Science equipment

The new labs in Le Centre Brown are kitted with cutting-edge equipment and materials.

$150K

Technology rollout

Middle School Chromebooks and Smartboards, robots for STEAM curriculum, and AI tools for responsible use are in student hands.

$120K

Faculty development

Faculty and staff are benefiting from IFSEL and EMPOWER conferences, as well as various mentoring and adult learning programs.

$25K

WISFIT equipment

WIS’s new dedicated fitness center is a major investment in faculty and student physical wellness.
